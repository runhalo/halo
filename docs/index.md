# Halo Documentation

**Open-source COPPA 2.0 risk scanner & ethical design linter**

Halo scans your codebase for potential children's privacy risks and ethical design issues. One command, 25 rules, 15+ languages.

```bash
npx runhalo scan .
```

## Why Halo?

COPPA 2.0 takes effect April 22, 2026. Non-compliance carries penalties of up to $53,088 per violation per day. Halo helps development teams surface potential privacy risks early, before code ships to production.

> **Note:** Halo is a developer tool designed to assist with code analysis and identifying potential privacy issues. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee compliance with COPPA, GDPR, or any other regulation. Always consult with qualified legal counsel regarding your specific compliance obligations.
